# The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Switch 2): azmasm's build

- **Code:** branch [`ocarina`](https://github.com/azmasm/hermit-builds/tree/ocarina) of this repository.
- **Game:** the Switch 2 release (title ID `0400486024DEC000`, update v131072), from your own dump.
- **What it is:** nxnative with Ocarina of Time pull requests merged ahead of nxnative itself: faster foliage, fewer stutters, a first start in seconds, picture fixes, and settings for frame generation, Fast mode, render scale and the full docked picture. The authors of each change are credited on the branch and in [Hermit's NOTICE.md](https://github.com/azmasm/hermit/blob/main/NOTICE.md).
- **How it runs:** recommended on M1 and M2 Macs with 32 GB or more. Other Macs get nxnative's own build in Hermit by default.
- **In Hermit:** [the game's page](https://github.com/azmasm/hermit/tree/main/games/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time), with its other builds and players' reports. Its profile is [`dm/games/0400486024DEC000`](https://github.com/azmasm/hermit-builds/tree/ocarina/dm/games/0400486024DEC000) on the branch.

To build it, use Hermit: choose your dump, press Build.
